Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · AgentLane 0.1.1

AgentLane is an open-source Chrome extension and a separate local native bridge/MCP server. It lets a user-directed AI client view and operate an owned browser tab using the Chrome profile's existing logins. AgentLane does not operate an AI service, require an AgentLane account, or run a developer-hosted screenshot collection service.

Data processed for your tasks

  • Page screenshots and website content: screenshots of the agent-owned tab are returned when your MCP client requests them. They may show names, email, messages, documents, account information, or other personal and sensitive data present on the page, including financial, health, authentication or location information.
  • Requested URLs and activity: the bridge processes the URLs, screenshot requests, clicks, scrolls, keys, and text requested by your MCP client. Typed or submitted information can be private. AgentLane does not independently record your general browsing history, physical keyboard, mouse activity, or other applications.
  • Local settings: Chrome stores your selected/any starting-website mode, selected origins, and the version of the disclosure you accepted in extension-local storage. The native helper uses local installation files and an owner-only Unix socket.

The tool interface cannot select pre-existing human tabs and does not expose cookie export, password-store extraction, clipboard access, or arbitrary page-script execution. Logins remain in Chrome; screenshots and actions still carry the authority and information visible in the signed-in page.

Where information goes

The extension sends tool responses, including screenshots, to the native bridge on your computer. The bridge passes them to your configured MCP client. That client may transmit screenshots, requested URLs, instructions, and typed text to its configured AI/model providers and retain them in conversations, logs, or tool history. Those recipients depend on the client and provider you choose; review their privacy policies and retention settings before use. The project does not choose a model provider or supply a hosted processing endpoint.

Websites you visit receive normal browser requests and any information or actions you submit to them, using your existing browser identity. Separate agent input does not isolate account changes from your own session. AgentLane's project website and repository are hosted by GitHub, whose hosting services may process ordinary visit information independently of the extension.

Purpose, sharing and limited use

Information is processed only to provide the user-directed visual browser tasks and the local configuration/connection needed for them. AgentLane does not implement advertising, analytics, sale of user data, credit scoring, or a developer-operated model-training pipeline. Transfers through your chosen MCP client and AI providers are necessary for the task you direct; they are not a claim that information stays on your device.

AgentLane's use of information received through Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Task information is not used for unrelated advertising, sale, or unrelated secondary purposes. AI clients and providers are separately configured by you; their independent data practices are governed by their own policies.

Storage and retention

The extension and bridge process screenshots and requests in memory and do not implement a persistent screenshot history. Settings remain locally until you change them or remove the extension. Optional development/demo scripts save test artifacts; those are separate from normal extension operation. MCP clients and model providers may retain conversation or tool data under their own settings and policies. We cannot delete copies held by those services on your behalf.

Your choices and controls

  • Review the in-product disclosure and affirm it before the extension establishes the browser bridge.
  • Choose Selected websites to restrict direct opening/navigation by exact origin, or explicitly choose Any website for HTTP(S) navigation. Links and redirects can lead elsewhere; this setting does not filter all page requests.
  • Stop requesting tools, close the agent-owned tab/window, disconnect the MCP client, or disable the extension to stop its workflow. AgentLane pauses input while the lane is focused or minimized. Native browser prompts and popups remain experimental isolation boundaries.
  • Change starting-site settings locally. Remove the extension to remove its Chrome-managed local settings, and follow the documented uninstall steps to remove the local native bridge and MCP registration. Manage any retained conversation or provider data with those services.

Security and contact

The native bridge communicates through Chrome native messaging and an owner-only local Unix socket, without opening a debugging TCP port. Debugger permission is broad: AgentLane is trusted local software, not a security sandbox against malicious extensions, sites, or local processes. Read the security model before sensitive use.

For privacy questions, use the project's issue tracker to request contact. Do not post private screenshots, emails, passwords, access tokens, or account details in a public issue. The project currently has no dedicated private support inbox.

Updates

Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this page and, where required, in the extension disclosure and store listing before the changed processing is enabled.
